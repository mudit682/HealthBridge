About PRAS

PRAS is a comprehensive platform designed to facilitate efficient resource management and coordination between government bodies, healthcare facilities, and citizens during health emergencies. The platform provides real-time updates on medical resource availability, predictive analytics for demand forecasting, and seamless communication channels for emergency response.
